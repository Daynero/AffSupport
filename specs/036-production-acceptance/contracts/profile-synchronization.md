# Account profile synchronization — T172

Physical Chrome reproduction on2026-10-23: the same local test account was open in two incognito tabs. Completing onboarding in one tab left the other on onboarding until a reload. A regression also demonstrated an old actor update response overwriting a newly signed-in actor profile.

The web client publishes only a random invalidation revision under a key scoped to the Supabase project origin after an acknowledged own-profile write. It never puts profile values, actor identifiers or credentials in the signal. The recipient queries its own authenticated RLS profile; message content is not profile data or authority. Focus and visible-page activation provide recovery when storage signals are unavailable or were missed.

At most one background request per provider is active, with one coalesced follow-up. A10second abort bounds each read. A failed read preserves the usable cached screen and retries on later activation. Unmount removes listeners and aborts its read. Actor, login intent, identity load and profile write revisions fence delayed results; a read started during a write cannot overwrite its completed result. A superseded write may have committed on the server, but cannot publish into a replacement identity. Signout invalidates profile work separately from login-intent ownership so its own journal and handoff cleanup still completes.

Diagnostics use separate `account.profile_update` and `account.profile_refresh` attempts with typed generic operation events. The closed causes are PROFILE_UPDATE_FAILED, PROFILE_REFRESH_FAILED and PROFILE_REFRESH_TIMEOUT. Source kind is form, sibling, focus or visible. Correlation includes attempt and flow IDs and uses the existing actor fence. Source-update success does not prove sibling receipt; recipient refresh is an independent attempt. Browser paint, inactive-tab inventory and reliable cross-tab delivery remain explicitly unobservable. Missing terminals after identity replacement or delivery loss remain partial in audit and inspect; another actor terminal must never close the original attempt. No name, email, language, newsletter choice, content or credential is collected in these events.
