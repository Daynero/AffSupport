# Restitch storage and executor compatibility

The service-only selection store is implemented and applied to local beta438.
It is an integration foundation: no public root initializer, protected range
reader, new native capability or product fallback path is enabled. T016–T020
remain open. N/delayed publication retains its separate provider-ownership block.
No production change or packaged-beta acceptance is claimed.

Three private forced-RLS tables bind an existing manual download/restitch
operation to immutable source/team/actor/recipe context, two bounded slots and
frozen candidate descriptors. Automated updater/Launch roots are refused until
immutable logical preparations and executor aliases are implemented. Download
roots gain no process/upload right. Exact CAS acknowledgement is required before
a range may dispatch. A lost reservation reply permits readback only. Trusted
bookkeeping can settle an issued range after cancellation/access revocation;
lost settlement acknowledgement may be confirmed against the exact measurement.
Missing, foreign or malformed state never becomes a fresh budget.

one per-file/two total refreshes. Administrative resets, confirmed-history edits
and late pool-completeness changes are rejected. A causal test reproduced a used
budget reset before this guard. Two additional causal failures reproduced lost
unvalidated prefixes: reacquisition now retains costs and retry history without
inventing validation. Settled prior work is required; pending ranges still block.
